In ther  Report of the High-Level Commission on Carbon Prices, one critical feature mentioned was the 'switching price' needed to transition from one technology to another for mass adoption. Such a definition includes naturally for example feebates and even consistent subisidies This sort of focus on the price within a sector makes sense for two reasons: first, large changes in revenue raising carbon pricing in coal-dependent sectors are politically difficult (see US for example); second, the elasticity tends to be higher within the sector (substituting between two options). Could a way to extend this work to be think about effective carbon pricing within each sector - and then to extend to 'effectiveness weighted policy'?
