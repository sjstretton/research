# 1.5 Instrument Diagnostic: A Six-Driver Framework

Status: no draft yet. Abstract in `research.qmd`.

Six drivers for matching climate policy instruments to country contexts:
fiscal pressure, political salience, vested interests, distributive design,
institutional quality, energy structure.

Applied to carbon taxes, emissions trading, feebates, long-term contracts,
standards, green public investment — producing a sequencing logic for
country-level advice.
