# 4.2 The Climate Policy Assessment Tool: Architecture, Recoding, and Fiscal Application

Status: in development (++). Abstract in `research.qmd`.

One paper, three strands:

1. **Architecture** — core mitigation structure of CPAT, power sector detail,
   policy interaction, country applications. Replaces the hard renewable-energy
   investment-flow constraint with system-adjusted marginal costs and shadow
   prices.
2. **Recoding** — rebuilding the price and demand logic as a transparent,
   testable kernel with AI assistance for translation and verification, and what
   that implies for how policy models should be built and maintained.
3. **Fiscal application** — carrying output into fiscal strategy via a
   SiSePuede–CCDR–CPAT framing: investment, revenues, fiscal risk, debt
   sustainability.
