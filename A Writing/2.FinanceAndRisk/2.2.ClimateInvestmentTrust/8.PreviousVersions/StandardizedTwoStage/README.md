# Split between 2.1 and 2.4

"A Solar Economy Financing System" (Stephen Stretton, Jasper Zimmermann) made two
proposals that have been separated, because they address different risks and
belong in different papers.

**Part (i), the multi-tiered guarantee structure for green bonds**, is now
section 3.4 of **2.1 Climate Finance Intermediation for Clean Power**, as the
case for system assets that have no power purchase agreement to guarantee —
transmission, charging networks, equipment factories. The conditionality and
the local-currency point went with it.

**Part (ii), regional clean energy equity funds**, is now
**2.4 Equity De-risking for Clean Energy**.

The reason for splitting rather than merging: 2.1 argues that guaranteeing the
offtake contract is better than guaranteeing the debt, because it is systemic
rather than transactional and preserves recourse. This paper guaranteed the
debt. The two are not in conflict once the domain is stated — guarantee the
contract where there is one, guarantee the bond where there is not — but they
are different mechanisms and were being described as one.
